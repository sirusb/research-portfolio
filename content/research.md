# Research

My research program centers on how computational methods, AI systems, and scientific workflows can be used to improve the reliability and interpretability of modern analysis pipelines.

## Current Interests

- Applied AI for biomedical and scientific research
- Evaluation frameworks for LLMs and decision-support systems
- Reproducible research, data pipelines, and transparent analysis
- Bridging computational methods with domain-specific research questions

## Research Philosophy

I aim to develop research that is rigorous, methodologically sound, and useful to scientific collaborators. I value interpretable systems, clear documentation, and workflows that support reproducibility in both academia and applied research.

## Ongoing Work

- Research in AI-assisted scientific analysis
- Data-centric evaluation of model performance and reliability
- Computational approaches for understanding complex scientific data
